Quick rules

  • Yellow line or No Stopping sign: don't stop at all, even for a second.
  • Loading zone: up to 2 minutes to drop off or pick up passengers, unless the sign says commercial vehicles only.
  • No Parking: up to 2 minutes for passengers if you stay within 3 m of the car.
  • Bus and taxi zones: never. Rideshare isn't a taxi.
