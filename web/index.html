      About this map

      Where rideshare drivers can and can't stop to drop off a passenger in inner Brisbane, built from Brisbane City Council's open parking data. Tap any line for details.

      Covers

      Brisbane City, Fortitude Valley, Newstead, Teneriffe, New Farm, Spring Hill, Herston

      Quick rules and fines

      • Yellow line or No Stopping sign: don't stop at all, even for a second. $345.
      • Clearway in its hours: no stopping, tow-away. $518.
      • Bus and taxi zones: never. Rideshare isn't a taxi. $345.
      • Loading zone: up to 2 minutes to drop off or pick up passengers, unless it's commercial vehicles only.
      • No Parking: up to 2 minutes for passengers if you stay within 3 m of the car.
      • Corners: no stopping within 10 m of an intersection, 20 m at traffic lights, unless a sign says otherwise. Not drawn on the map yet.

      Parking cameras

      Blue-shaded streets have a council parking camera somewhere along them, as announced by council or reported in the news. A white camera pin means the exact position isn't confirmed; a filled blue pin is a confirmed position. Cameras run 24/7 and catch illegal stops: no stopping zones and yellow lines, bus and taxi zones, clearways, and misuse of loading zones (over time, or commercial-only).

      How it's built

      • Red and green lines come from council's painted line records and its sign records. Where there's only a sign, the map runs the rule along the kerb in the direction of the sign's arrow, to the next sign or the corner.
      • Timed rules follow the time at the top of the screen. Public holidays aren't accounted for: on a holiday, weekday-only rules may not apply and "all other times" rules may. Check the sign.
      • Green is only shown when the data is clear. Loading zones with unclear hours, or that conflict with another record, show amber instead.
      • Not drawn: the corner rule, driveways, roads council doesn't manage (hospital campuses like RBWH, private car parks), and anything missing from council's data.
